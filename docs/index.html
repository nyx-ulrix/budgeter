BUDGETER

A pixel-art budget planner for Android. See what you can spend today, scan receipts and split them fairly down to the service charge and GST, plan trips and save for them month by month, and keep an optional copy of everything in your own Google Sheet.

  • Daily budget that adjusts itself after you overspend
  • Receipt scanning on your phone, with per-item tax and service charge
  • Bill splitting for groups: equal, percent, shares, exact or by item
  • Trip budgets with planned costs and a monthly set-aside
  • Home-screen widgets, including small ones for flip-phone cover screens
