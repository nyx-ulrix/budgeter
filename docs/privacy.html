BUDGETER · PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated 13 October 2026

The short version

Budgeter keeps your budget on your phone. It has no account, no server of its own, no ads and no analytics. Data only leaves the phone when you turn on a feature that needs it, and then only to the service you chose.

What stays on your phone

  • Your budget, expenses, trips, groups and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Without an AI provider, receipt photos and screenshots are read on the phone with on-device text recognition and never uploaded. The photo is deleted from the phone after reading either way.
  • AI provider keys you add are encrypted with the Android Keystore and never leave the phone except to the provider they belong to.
  • The web app (app.budgeter.liewjiaen.com) keeps the same data in your browser's own storage on that device instead. Its AI keys are encrypted with a key the browser keeps and can't export. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes it. There is no Budgeter server.

What is sent, and only when you use it

  • Google Sheets sync (optional). If you connect Google, Budgeter keeps one spreadsheet named "Budgeter" in your Google Drive: a readable tab per month with your transactions (date, merchant, category, amounts, payer, split method, trip names, items and notes), and a hidden tab holding your budget, planned purchases, trips, categories, meals, food presets, macro goals and settings, so the phone app and the web app can share them. The web app also asks Google for your email address, only to remember which account to sign in again with. It uses the drive.file permission, which only lets it see files it created. It does not read any other Drive or Google data. You can disconnect at any time in Profile → Google Sheets sync, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • AI receipt reading (optional). If you add an AI provider, the receipt photo or screenshot you scan is sent to that provider (for example Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek or Perplexity) using your own key, to read it into items, tax and service charge. If the provider's model can't read images, the text recognised on your phone is sent instead. That provider's own privacy policy applies to what you send it.
  • AI macros (optional). If you tap Look up or Fill all while adding food, the food name you typed (for example "whey protein") is sent to your AI provider with your own key, to estimate its calories and nutrients. Nothing else is sent.
  • Exchange rates. For spending in another currency, the app asks open.er-api.com for the day's rates. Only currency codes are sent.
  • Update checks. The app asks GitHub for the latest Budgeter release and downloads it from GitHub when you choose to update. No personal data is sent.

Google user data

Budgeter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to keep your own budget in your own spreadsheet and sync it between your own devices. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone else and not read by people.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Budgeter, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored on the phone. The spreadsheet in your Drive is yours: delete it there if you no longer want it.

Children

Budgeter is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/nyx-ulrix/budgeter/issues.
